Privacy Policy

Vanity Jam, last updated October 28, 2026

Vanity Jam is a puzzle game made by Kettlekite, the studio name of independent developer Mert Can Gözükara. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for an account, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, the game engine's own analytics and crash reporting are switched off, and it never sends anything to us. We run no server for it.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, stars, gems, boosters, your win streak, the daily gift and your sound, music, vibration and reminder settings) is saved only on your device. So are your profile (the nickname you choose, your avatar, frame and achievements), your progress in events and the Glam Pass, and the hours at which you played lately, which reminders use. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Events run on your device. Their rivals are computer-controlled, and your nickname and scores are not sent anywhere.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include this data, under Apple's terms. We never see it.

Game Center

From version 1.4, the game uses Game Center, Apple's game service, for a Highest Level leaderboard and for inviting friends. Game Center is optional: the whole game can be played without it.

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game signs in when it starts (Apple may show its welcome banner), and the level you have reached goes to the game's leaderboard at launch and after each level you win. Game Center shows that score according to your Game Center settings.

Invite opens Apple's friend request sheet, or Apple's sign-in sheet if you are not signed in. If neither works, it opens the share sheet with the game's App Store link; Share Link sends just the link. You choose who receives it. Only when you tap See Friends does iOS ask whether the game may see your Game Center friends. If you allow it, the game reads which of your friends play Vanity Jam and the level they have reached, shows them on your device, and keeps the game-specific Game Center IDs of the friends who earned you a free revive, so that each friend counts once. If you decline, the rest of the game works the same. You can change your answer later in iOS Settings.

Your Game Center account, friends and scores are held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The game does not send them to us.

Notifications and permissions

The game can ask for two permissions, and both are optional; it never asks during play.

Reminders: only when you turn on reminders in the game's Settings does iOS ask whether the game may send notifications. If you allow it, the game schedules a notification for your next daily gift on your device, at the hour you usually play. That hour is worked out on your device from your own play times and is never sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Game Center friends: only when you tap See Friends, as described above.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Gems and boosters you buy are saved with your progress on your device, together with the numbers of your recent purchases, so that a purchase is never added twice.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases can be limited with Screen Time and Ask to Buy, and Game Center features such as adding friends with Screen Time.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change accordingly.
