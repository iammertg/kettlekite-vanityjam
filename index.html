How it plays

Three rules, no timers. Play at your own pace.

  1. Tap to sort

    Tap a lipstick, a cream or a hair dryer and it flies to its box: Hair, Skincare or Makeup.

  2. Top comes first

    Anything lying on top has to go before what's under it. Press and hold an item to look underneath.

  3. Mind the tray

    Items no box wants yet wait in a five-slot tray. Let it fill up and the drawer wins this one.

Twenty real products, three boxes.

Each one is modelled from a real product, printed labels and all, so you can tell where it goes at a glance.
