A calm 3D sorting puzzle for iPhone. Tap a lipstick, a brush or a palette and it flies into its organiser box.

How it plays

Hair tools go to Hair, jars and tubes to Skincare, colour to Makeup. Anything lying on top has to go first; press and hold an item to see what's underneath. Items no box wants yet wait in a five-slot holding tray, so don't let it fill up.

200 levels, from relaxing to truly tricky, and every one is guaranteed solvable. No timers: play at your own pace.

For parents

No account, no ads and no analytics. Progress stays on the iPhone. Purchases go through the App Store. The privacy policy has the details.
