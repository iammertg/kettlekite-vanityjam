Support

Vanity Jam, a cosy makeup-drawer sorting puzzle for iPhone.

Write to support@kettlekite.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your levels, stars, gems and boosters.

How do I see what's under an item?

Press and hold it. Anything lying on top has to go first.

How do I turn reminders off?

Open Settings in the game and turn Reminders off, or go to iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Vanity Jam.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
